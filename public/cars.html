Cars and upgrades in Skidline

There are five cars, each with its own handling and engine sound. You start with the Vanta GT and buy the other four with coins from races. Every car takes eight upgrade parts with three stages each, and a stage changes the numbers the physics runs on, so an upgraded car really is quicker on the road.

The five cars

Vanta GT: free, your first car

"Balanced. Nothing to fear, nothing it is best at." Every other car in the game is the Vanta with a few numbers changed, so it is the one to learn on. It sounds like a high, smooth twelve-cylinder.

Kite RS: 6,000 coins

"Light hatch with sticky tyres. Slow on the straights, untouchable in the bends." It has the lowest top speed of the five but the most grip and the most steering lock, and it rates lowest for drifting. It sounds like a buzzy turbo four with a whistle that rises with boost.

Brute V8: 14,000 coins

"Muscle car. Huge top speed and a rear end that wants to come round." The highest top speed of the five and the hardest nitro push, but the least grip and a little less pull than the Vanta. It rates highest for drifting. It sounds like a low, lumpy eight with a loping idle.

Volt E: 26,000 coins

"Electric. Instant pull off every corner and a nitro tank that lasts." The strongest pull of the five and the slowest-burning nitro tank, though each burst pushes a little less hard than the Vanta's. It sounds like an electric motor's whine.

Kestrel S: 20,000 coins

"Light sports coupe. Turns in sharper than any of the others and pulls hard, but it skates when pushed." It turns in quickest of the five, has as much steering lock as the Kite, more pull than the Vanta and the second-highest top speed, 270 km/h, which puts it in class A from the start. It can corner a little harder than the Vanta, but its tyres let go sooner once it starts to slide, so it skates more readily. It is the sports car from the traffic pack, painted in your colour, and it sounds like a turbo four.

Upgrades

Open the Garage and switch to the Upgrades tab. Each part has three stages, bought in order and only for a car you own. A stage costs a share of the car's price, so cheap cars are cheap to tune. The Vanta GT is free, so it is priced as if it cost 10,000. On the Kite RS, for example, engine stage 1 costs 900 coins and stage 3 costs 3,000.

Classes and rating

Every car has a class from its top speed, with its upgrades counted: S from 295 km/h, A from 270, B from 245, C from 220 and D below that. Stock, the Brute V8 is class A, the Vanta GT and the Volt E are B, and the Kite RS is C. Upgrades can lift a car a class: a stage 3 engine takes the Vanta GT to 290 km/h and class A, the Kite RS to 266 km/h and class B, and the Brute V8 to 314 km/h and class S.

Next to the class the garage shows a rating from 100 to 999. It weighs top speed most, then pull and grip, then braking, and every upgrade that touches those raises it.

Looks

The garage's Looks tab has paint finishes (gloss, matte, pearl and chrome) and underglow colours. Try one on the turntable before you buy it. A look is bought once and then works on every car you own. Prices are on the coins page.
