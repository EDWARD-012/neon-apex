Coins: how to earn them and what they buy

Every event in Neon Apex pays coins, and coins buy cars and upgrades. After each race the results screen lists every line of the payout.

Those race, drift and time trial figures are for three laps. One lap pays a third of them and five laps five thirds, rounded to the nearest 50, so short races are not the quick way to get rich.

Career events

A career event pays its own reward instead of the table above: the full amount for the first win, a quarter for a repeat win, and part of it for a result short of a win. The modes page lists every event and its reward.

Getting busted, in a pursuit or by patrol cars in a race, pays nothing at all, but it takes nothing away either. A wrecked car keeps what it earned on the way but gets nothing for its place.

The daily track bonus

The first run of each day on today's track pays a bonus: 1,000 coins, plus 250 for every day in a row you have come back, up to 2,500 a day from the seventh day on. Days follow the UTC calendar. Today's track is a quick race; career events don't count for it.

What coins buy

  • Cars: the Kite RS for 6,000, the Brute V8 for 14,000 and the Volt E for 26,000. The Vanta GT is yours from the start.
  • Upgrades: eight parts with three stages each, priced as a share of the car's price. See the cars page.

Coins are saved in your browser. Sign in from Account in the menu to keep them on every device.
