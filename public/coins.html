Coins: how to earn them and what they buy

Every event in Skidline pays coins, and coins buy cars and upgrades. After each race the results screen lists every line of the payout.

Those race, drift and time trial figures are for three laps. One lap pays a third of them and five laps five thirds, rounded to the nearest 50, so short races are not the quick way to get rich.

Career events

A career event pays its own reward instead of the table above: the full amount for the first win, a quarter for a repeat win, and part of it for a result short of a win. The modes page lists every event and its reward.

Career medals pay on top, once each, the first time you reach them: bronze 500, silver 1,000, gold 2,000 and the Skidline medal 5,000. Going straight to gold pays for bronze and silver too. Beating a crew leader the first time lets you pick a reward card: the leader's prize in coins again (5,000, 9,000 or 18,000), a free upgrade stage, or a look.

Getting busted, in a pursuit or by patrol cars in a race, pays nothing at all, but it takes nothing away either. A wrecked car keeps what it earned on the way but gets nothing for its place.

The daily track bonus

The first run of each day on today's track pays a bonus: 1,000 coins, plus 250 for every day in a row you have come back, up to 2,500 a day from the seventh day on. Days follow the UTC calendar. Today's track is a quick race; career events don't count for it.

This week's track and the daily challenge

There is also a track for each week, Monday to Sunday by UTC, the same for every player; pick This week's track on the Quick race screen. Your first finished run on it each week pays 1,000 coins.

Every day has a challenge, the same for everyone, shown on the Quick race screen: win a quick race, score 2,500 drift points in one event, escape a pursuit at heat 2 or more, make 10 near misses in one event, bust 3 racers as the cop, finish a race of 3 laps or more without a scratch, or finish on the podium in an elimination. Meeting it pays 1,500 coins, once a day.

What coins buy

  • Cars: the Kite RS for 6,000, the Brute V8 for 14,000 and the Volt E for 26,000. The Vanta GT is yours from the start.
  • Upgrades: eight parts with three stages each, priced as a share of the car's price. See the cars page.
  • Looks, in the garage's Looks tab: a matte finish for 1,500, pearl for 3,000 and chrome for 6,000, underglow in cyan, pink, green, orange or white for 1,500 each, and liveries: twin stripes or a centre stripe for 2,000, a side flash or a chequered band for 2,500, and race numbers for 3,000. Each look is bought once and then works on every car you own. Gloss paint, the paint colours, an underglow in the paint colour, the livery's colour and your race number are free.
  • Repairs: damage from a career event stays on your car for the next career event until you repair it on the career screen. A full repair, from wrecked to new, costs 2,000 coins, and less damage costs its share of that. However bad it got, the car starts an event with at most half damage. Quick races, today's track and this week's track always give you a fresh car.

Coins are saved in your browser. Sign in from Account in the menu to keep them on every device.
