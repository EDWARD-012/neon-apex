Game modes and the career

Skidline has five kinds of event. Play any of them as a quick race on any track, or work through the career, where twelve events in three tiers pay coins and open up as you win.

Play Skidline

Race

Five rivals, and the first car across the line after the last lap wins. The rivals drive a racing line worked out for each track. A lap only counts when you pass all sixteen checkpoints in order, so there are no shortcuts. With police on, a patrol car also waits by the road; the police page explains what happens if it sees you. Shove a rival into a wall hard enough and it is wrecked, which is a takedown.

Elimination

Every time the leader finishes a lap, the car in last place is out. Keep going until you are the last car running. Police can wait by the road here too.

Drift

On your own, with the laps counting down. Score as many drift points as you can; in a quick race the target is 900 points a lap. The drift guide explains how points are scored.

Pursuit

The police are on you from the start, two patrol cars in a quick race. There are no laps: get 300 m clear of every patrol car and stay there long enough to escape. The longer it goes on, the higher the heat and the more police join. See police chases for the full rules.

Time trial

On your own against a ghost of your best lap on the track. In a quick race the target is a lap within 12% of the reference lap, the time the computed racing line plans for that track.

Quick race options

  • Track: today's track, which is new every day and the same for every player, or a new random track. A random track's link opens the same track for anyone you send it to.
  • Laps: 1, 3 or 5.
  • Traffic: on or off.
  • Police: on or off, for races and eliminations. With it on, a patrol car waits by the road and up to two can chase you.
  • Weather: Day puts you on a mountain road through a forest with snowy peaks behind it. Night, Rain and Dusk are in the city.

The career

Twelve events in three tiers: Street, Downtown and Apex. Only Street is open at first. Win three of its four events to open Downtown, and three in Downtown to open Apex.

Every race and elimination in the career has police waiting by the road: up to one patrol car can chase you in Street, two in Downtown and three in Apex. Getting busted or wrecked ends the event with nothing for your place.

Each event pays its reward in coins the first time you win it. Winning it again pays a quarter, so one event can't be farmed. Falling short still pays something: second place in a race or elimination pays half, third pays 30% and anything lower 10%. A drift event pays up to 40% depending on how close you got to the target, a missed time trial pays 15%, and getting busted in a pursuit pays nothing. Near misses, drift points and clean laps pay on top, as the coins page explains.
