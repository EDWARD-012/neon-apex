How to drift in Neon Apex

Drifts in Neon Apex are made to be easy to start and easy to hold. A slide scores points, fills the nitro tank, charges a boost, and in drift events it is the whole game. It all runs in the browser, with nothing to download.

Starting a drift

At speed, turn the wheel hard and tap the brake. The tail swings out and the car is drifting. Space does the same, as does the Drift button on a phone or B on a gamepad. The brake tap only works with plenty of speed and the wheel turned well over; otherwise the brake just slows you down. This is the Drift assist in Settings, which is on by default; with it off, Space is a plain handbrake and the tyres decide the rest.

Holding it

Keep the throttle down. It holds your speed through the slide, the way spinning rear wheels would. Then steer with the angle:

  • Steer into the corner and the angle grows from about 20 degrees to about 40, and the car's line tightens.
  • Countersteer and the angle drops to a few degrees, and the line opens out.
  • Lift off the throttle and the angle sags while the car scrubs off speed. Braking slows it more.

Ending it

Straighten the wheel for a moment. The angle comes down and the tyres grip again. A drift also ends on its own if the car slows down too much or hits a wall hard.

Drift boost

Holding a drift charges a boost. After one second the sparks off the back wheels turn blue, and after three seconds they turn pink. Straighten up while you still have speed and the charge comes out as a push a little past the car's normal top speed: 0.6 seconds of it for blue, 1.2 seconds for pink. A hard hit on a wall during the drift loses the charge.

Drift points

Points build up while the car slides, faster the quicker you go and the wider the angle. They are banked a moment after the slide ends, so a new slide started straight away keeps adding to the same score. Slides worth less than 30 points don't count, and a hard knock against another car mid-slide loses the points you had not banked yet.

At the end of a race every 10 drift points pay 1 coin, in any kind of event.

Drifting fills the nitro

Sliding is the quickest way to refill the nitro tank, and a wider angle fills it faster. A good habit is to drift through a corner and burn the nitro on the straight after it.

Which car drifts best

Each car has a Drift bar in the garage. The Brute V8 rates highest, with a rear end that wants to come round, and the Kite RS, with its sticky tyres, rates lowest. The Vanta GT you start with sits in between. More on each car on the cars page.

Drift events

In a quick race, pick Drift as the event. You race alone and the target is 900 points a lap; reaching it pays the win. The career has two drift events:

  • Wet paint, in the Street tier: 1,800 points in two laps on a wet road.
  • Sideways, in the Apex tier: 6,000 points in three laps at dusk.
