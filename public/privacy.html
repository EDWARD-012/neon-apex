Neon Apex privacy

You can play Neon Apex without an account. Your settings, career progress, cars and ghost laps are then kept only in your browser's local storage, on your device.

If you sign in with Google, the game receives your Google account's ID number, email address and whether that address is verified. It stores the ID number and the email address so it can recognise you next time. It does not see your Google password and asks for nothing else.

On your first sign-in you choose a username and a password for signing in on other devices. The password is stored only as a salted scrypt hash, never as written.

For a signed-in player the server also stores the career save: coins, cars owned, upgrades fitted, events won and the daily-track streak. That is all it stores. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and the game has no analytics or tracking scripts.

A cookie named na_session keeps you signed in for up to 90 days. Signing out removes it.

The game runs on Vercel and the database on Neon. To have your account deleted, email ravi5258p@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be removed.
