Neon Apex: a free online racing game in your browser

Neon Apex is a 3D street racing game that runs in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to download or install. Race AI rivals round a mountain road through pine forest and snowy peaks by day, or through a neon city in the rain at night. Drift to fill your nitro, thread through traffic, get away from the police, and drive from the chase camera or from the driver's seat.

What you can do

  • Play a career of twelve events across three tiers, earning cash to buy and paint new cars.
  • Race five AI rivals that drive a racing line worked out for each track.
  • Play elimination races, drift events with a points target, police pursuits and time trials against your own ghost.
  • Race a new track every day, the same for every player, or share a random track by link.
  • Crash and see the bodywork dent where you hit; badly damaged cars lose power, smoke and burn.
  • Switch between six cameras, including a cockpit view with a working steering wheel.
  • Choose a sunny mountain road or a city at night in clear weather, rain or at dusk.

The cars

Four cars to drive: a mid-engined hypercar, a light coupe with sticky tyres, a long-nosed GT that loves to slide, and an electric car with instant pull. Each has its own engine sound and its own handling, and every car's grip comes from a tyre model, so drifts are started with the handbrake and held with throttle and countersteer. Traffic and the police drive ten everyday cars: sedans, hatchbacks, wagons, vans, SUVs and pickups.

Questions

Is Neon Apex free?
Yes. There is nothing to buy and no ads.
Do I need to download anything?
No. It runs in the browser with WebGL. It is tested in Chrome, Firefox and WebKit, the engine behind Safari, and works best with a graphics card; the Low graphics setting helps on older machines.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes, with on-screen buttons. Turn the phone sideways; on Android it goes full screen when a race starts.
Can I play with a keyboard or a gamepad?
Both. Arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space to drift, Shift for nitro, C to change camera, V to look back.
How is my progress saved?
In your browser automatically. Sign in with Google, then pick a username and password, to keep your cash, cars and career on every device.
Is there multiplayer?
Not yet. Every race is against the computer, the police or your own ghost.
