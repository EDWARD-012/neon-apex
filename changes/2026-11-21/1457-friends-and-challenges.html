• Friends and challenges. Add drivers by their username on the new Friends screen, and see who added you so you can add them back. When one of your friends beats your time-trial lap on a board, the menu shows it under Rivals with a button to race their ghost. Once you have added each other, you can send a friend the track of a checked lap with your time as a challenge; challenges wait on the Friends screen with Race and Dismiss. Friends are for signed-in players, and the privacy page says what is stored.
